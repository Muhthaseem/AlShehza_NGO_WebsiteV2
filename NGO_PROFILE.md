## Purpose

Al Shehza Foundation works to ease poverty and hardship by supporting people and families with practical programmes related to essential needs. The current website describes work in community and household water access, food assistance, children’s nourishment, seasonal giving, and learning resources.

The public-facing purpose can be summarized as:

> To support people and families experiencing hardship through practical community programmes, offered with respect for every person’s dignity.

This is a summary of the current website copy, not a verified quotation from the foundation’s governing documents.

## Communities and people served

The initiatives described on the website are intended to support:

- People and households experiencing poverty or hardship.
- People using shared water facilities at mosques and madrasas.
- Children receiving food support or Quran learning resources.
- Community members receiving iftar meals or Qurban meat during relevant religious occasions.

The repository does not specify geographic service boundaries, programme eligibility criteria, referral procedures, or beneficiary totals. Those details should be confirmed before publication.

## Current programmes

The website presents seven initiatives.

### 1. Community well project

Community water tanks with piped water at mosques and madrasas support access to water for drinking and wudhu. These facilities serve as shared resources for people using those community spaces.

### 2. Tube-well donations

Household tube wells support access to water for drinking and daily activities at home.

### 3. Rice donations for households

Rice is donated to people and families experiencing hardship. The programme provides a staple food that households can use to prepare meals.

### 4. Iftar food donations

During Ramadhan, meals are prepared and shared with children and community members so they can break their fast.

### 5. Yoghurt donations for children

Yoghurt is shared with children as part of the foundation’s food and nourishment support. The repository does not provide clinical or nutritional outcome data.

### 6. Qurban programme

The foundation arranges Qurban and distributes the meat to people and families experiencing hardship during Eid al-Adha. Project photographs have not been supplied for the website.

### 7. Quran donations

Qurans are donated to children learning to read and write the Quran or pursuing memorisation. Project photographs have not been supplied for the website.

## Guiding values

The website describes three values that guide the foundation’s work:

- **Empathy:** Listen to people and respond with compassion to the circumstances they face.
- **Dignity:** Put people first and respect their worth, voice, and choices.
- **Responsibility:** Act thoughtfully, communicate honestly, and handle contributions with care.
